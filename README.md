# 🌿 Wildrune

**Wildrune** is a third-person fantasy action game developed in Unity where a young druid explores the wilds, discovers ancient runes, and unlocks powerful elemental magic.

Created as part of my **Game Design & Development studies at Toronto Film School**, Wildrune is an ongoing solo project focused on gameplay programming, combat systems, enemy behaviours, exploration, and player progression.

## 🎮 About the Game

The player begins their journey armed with a basic magical wand before discovering a powerful druid staff and ancient elemental runes.

As new abilities are unlocked, the player's combat options expand, allowing them to approach enemies using a growing collection of spells and abilities.

Wildrune was designed as a hands-on exploration of building interconnected gameplay systems in Unity while creating a polished and playable third-person experience.

## ✨ Key Features

* Third-person exploration and combat
* Wand and staff-based ranged combat
* Unlockable elemental rune abilities
* Enemy patrol, detection, chase, and combat behaviours
* Multiple enemy archetypes with unique attacks
* Lock-on targeting and target switching
* Character Controller-based movement
* Dodge and combat movement systems
* Player health and damage systems
* Enemy health and damage feedback
* Combat HUD with ability cooldowns
* Interactive pickups and progression
* Main menu, settings, pause, and credits systems
* Large outdoor environment built using modular assets and terrain tools

## 🔮 Elemental Abilities

The staff provides access to several unlockable abilities discovered through ancient runes, including:

**⚡ Lightning Strike**
Calls down lightning on a targeted enemy.

**❄️ Ice Tornado**
Creates an offensive ice-based area attack.

**🌿 Entangle**
Uses nature magic to restrain enemies.

Additional abilities and gameplay systems continue to be developed as the project evolves.

## 👾 Enemy Systems

Wildrune features multiple enemy archetypes designed around different combat behaviours.

Enemies can patrol their environments, detect and pursue the player, attack within defined ranges, take damage from player abilities, react to hits, and return to their patrol behaviour when the player leaves their detection area.

Enemy types include melee-focused enemies as well as ranged and ability-based opponents.

## 🧠 Development Focus

A major goal of Wildrune has been learning how individual gameplay features evolve into maintainable, interconnected systems.

Development has included iterative refactoring of player and enemy architecture, debugging interactions between combat systems, improving component responsibilities, and designing systems that can be expanded without tightly coupling unrelated gameplay features.

The project has also provided extensive hands-on experience identifying, reproducing, troubleshooting, and resolving gameplay bugs throughout development.
